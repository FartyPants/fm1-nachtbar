FM-1 NACHTBAR nightly 0.9.99-nightly

TEST BUILD, not a release. It is unfinished and changes often. Installing it replaces the firmware now on the FM-1. SAVE in the FM-1 NACHTBAR writes the place in the flash where other FM-1 firmwares keep their saved settings: theirs may be lost. To go back, install the other firmware again from its own installer; the installer can also put M-VAVE's stock firmware back.
